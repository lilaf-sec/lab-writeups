# Squid Proxies

---

## 📌 Définition
> **Squid Proxy vulnerabilities** usually come from misconfigured Squid proxy servers that allow unauthorized users to route traffic through them, access internal networks, or bypass network restrictions.

---

## 🛠️ Impact
- Access to internal services through proxying (pivoting)
- Bypassing firewall restrictions and network segmentation
- Exposure of internal resources if ACL rules are weak
