# SNMP - Simple Network Management Protocol

---

## 📌 Définition
> **SNMP (Simple Network Management Protocol)** is a network protocol used for monitoring and managing devices such as servers, routers, printers, and network appliances. Misconfigured SNMP services — especially with default community strings like `public` — can expose sensitive system information, credentials, and internal configuration data.

---

## 🛠️ Impact
- Disclosure of system information (users, processes, installed software)
- Exposure of plaintext or encoded credentials
- Access to network configuration details
- Enumeration of internal infrastructure
- Potential pivoting or direct service compromise
